03 / At a glance

What stays yours.

Accounts & assets

You keep what you own. If you stop, Ignite stops managing; live automations still need active accounts and tools.

Separate costs

Ad spend and any applicable software or provider fees are separate unless quoted otherwise.

Agreed scope

Your quote defines deliverables and access. Estimates are opportunities, not guaranteed jobs.
